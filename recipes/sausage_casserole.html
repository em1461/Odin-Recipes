Sausage Casserole


Description

Simple to put together, Sausage Cassoulet is a classic French comfort food made with slow roasted sausages, tomatoes and white beans.


Ingredients:

  • Italian sausages
  • cherry tomatoes
  • onion
  • garlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Balsamic vinegar
  • thyme, rosemary, and bay leaves
  • salt and pepper
  • cannellini beans

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F.
  2. In a roasting pan or large low sided cast iron pot, combine tomatoes, olive oil, onion, garlic, and spices together. Arrange cut sausages over the tomato mixture.
  3. Bake for 45 minutes until the tomatoes have burst and created a sauce and sausages are brown and cooked through.
  4. Add beans and stir to combine. Cook for an additional 5- 10 minutes until heated through.
  5. Remove bay leaves before serving.
