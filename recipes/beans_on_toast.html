Beans on Toast


Description

Simple to put together,beans on toast is a British staple. Have it for breakfast, lunch, or dinner!


Ingredients:

  • Baked beans
  • Butter
  • Bread
  • Cheese, optional

Steps:

  1. Open the tin of beans and place in a saucepan over medium heat. Simmer for 5 minutes until hot.
  2. Put 2 slices of bread in the toaster and toast to your preference.
  3. Once the bread has been toasted, put a generous amount of butter on it. Tip: Add the cheese on top of the buttered bread here so that the hot beans will melt it.
  4. Pour over your beans, and add cheese.
